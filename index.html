Method

New code is where the bugs live, so I start with a repository's most recent commits and work backwards until I understand what changed and why. Version diffs of security releases are the second entry point: what a vendor patched tells you what they missed.

Findings get reproduced locally before anything is reported. A bug I cannot demonstrate in the project's own development environment is not a finding, it is a theory.
